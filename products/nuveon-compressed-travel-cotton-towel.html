Short Description

This compressed travel cotton towel is designed for modern travellers who value convenience, hygiene and comfort. Packed into a compact size for easy carrying, the towel expands into a full-size bath towel once it comes into contact with water.

Made from soft plant-based fibers with a thick 90gsm fabric structure, it provides excellent absorbency and a gentle touch on skin. The large size ensures full-body coverage while remaining lightweight and space-saving for travel, gym, outdoor activities and everyday use.

Each towel is individually packed for cleanliness and portability, making it an ideal companion for travel, sports and outdoor adventures.
