Short Description

A modern touchless automatic toothpaste dispenser designed to make daily brushing cleaner, easier and more hygienic.
Using advanced infrared sensing technology, the dispenser automatically releases toothpaste when a toothbrush is detected. The stepless adjustment knob allows precise control of toothpaste amount, suitable for both adults and children.

Powered by a high-efficiency vacuum pump system, it ensures smooth and accurate dispensing every time. The device features Type-C rechargeable battery technology with ultra-long battery life, lasting up to six months on a single charge.

Installation is quick and simple with punch-free adhesive mounting, keeping your wall intact while maintaining a clean and modern bathroom look.
